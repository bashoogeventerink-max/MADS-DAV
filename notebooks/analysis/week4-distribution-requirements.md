# Week 4 — distribution chart requirements

Requirements for the lesson 4 (distributions) assignment chart, noted here
for later reference — not yet built.

1. **X-axis: all possible options shown.** Every outcome in the domain must
   appear on the axis, even ones with zero/near-zero observed count — e.g.
   a dice throw shows all six faces (1–6), not just the ones that happened
   to occur in the data.
2. **Y-axis: likelihood of each option.** The chart shows the probability/
   relative frequency of each outcome, not a raw count (unless raw count
   and likelihood are the same thing for the dataset in question).
3. **Story: a shift in distribution tied to an event in time.** The
   deliverable needs a *significant, explainable* change in the
   distribution's shape — not just a static single distribution — where the
   shift can be tied to and explained by something that happened at a
   specific point in time.
